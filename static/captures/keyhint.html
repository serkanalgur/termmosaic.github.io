form.NewKeyHint(r buffer.Rect, bindings []form.Binding) *form.KeyHint — widgets/form

A one-line bar of key names and their meanings.

At 40 columns the bar has fewer cells than bindings, so it drops the lowest-priority ones rather than truncating mid-word. That trade is visible in the narrow capture.
