ProgressBar

viz.NewProgressBar(r buffer.Rect) *viz.ProgressBar — widgets/viz

A ratio from 0 to 1 drawn as a filled block-element bar.

Filled with U+2588 FULL BLOCK. The difference between FillStyle and TrackStyle carries an attribute as well as a colour, so the bar is readable without either.

MinSize() = 14 × 3 cells
