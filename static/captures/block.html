Block

block.New(r buffer.Rect) *block.Block — widgets/block

A bordered, titled, padded rectangle that other widgets draw inside.

A Block draws chrome only — it has no children. What goes inside it is the application's business: wrap a widget, or use a Split to place several. The capture shows the padding and the interior band, which is the part of the widget that is actually drawn.
