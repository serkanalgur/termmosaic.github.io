Split

split.New(d layout.Direction, panes ...termmosaic.Widget) *split.Split — widgets/split

A container that divides its rectangle among focusable panes.

Focus moves between panes with Tab and the focused pane is the one that takes keys. A still frame cannot show that; the marker in the second pane's title is where the capture has to stop.

MinSize() = 3 × 1 cells
