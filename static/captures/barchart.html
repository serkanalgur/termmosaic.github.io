BarChart

viz.NewBarChart(r buffer.Rect) *viz.BarChart — widgets/viz

Categorical magnitudes as horizontal or vertical bars, with an axis and per-bar values.

Bars are Block Elements. The value beside each bar is the colour-independent reading and the capture keeps it.
