Sparkline

viz.NewSparkline(r buffer.Rect) *viz.Sparkline — widgets/viz

A series of numbers drawn as Braille or block elements, scaled to its own range.

Braille encoding, two samples per cell, so the series holds twice the values the width can show. The threshold defaults to reverse video, which is an attribute rather than a colour.

MinSize() = 5 × 3 cells

40 columns × 3 rows
